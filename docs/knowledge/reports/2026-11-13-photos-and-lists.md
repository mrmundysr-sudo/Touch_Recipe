## Numbered lists (owner request, same day)
Separate numbering for ingredients and steps. Paste keeps ALL pasted text in one entry (line breaks become spaces; nothing split at sentences or lines, nothing stripped). Enter splits at the exact cursor; Backspace at the very start joins to the entry above and its number goes (Delete at the very end joins the next). Select entries -> Delete / Replace with text, and Replace all, to remove a bad section, paste the right text as one entry and add breaks. Every split, join, paste, delete and replace is its own Undo step (typing before it is taken in as a separate step first); Ctrl/Cmd+Z works; the cursor returns after Undo/Redo. Tests: `tests/numbered.test.mjs`, `tools/e2e-rescue-plain.cjs`.

## Photos (owner requirements)
- **Find:** `ImportService` collects the pictures of the source and `engine/photopick.js` judges them. Rejected, never chosen on their own but still listed apart ("probably not the dish"): too small, banner or strip shaped (wider than 2.2:1 or taller than 1:0.45), carrying a see-through mask (adverts, logos), mostly one colour. Exactly one usable picture: used automatically. Several: the finished-dish photo is preferred (the page's own first choice, the photo printed on a recipe page) and the person is asked to check. None: no photo, and the person is told.
- **The owner's Veg-All PDF** contains ONE picture, an advertisement (750 x 300, with a soft mask), confirmed with `pdfimages -list`. The previous code cut it off the rendered page and used it as the recipe photo (reproduced). Now: found, rejected as a banner, **not used**, the person is told (`tools/e2e-real-pdf.cjs`, both themes).
- **PDFs:** embedded pictures are taken out directly by native code (`touchRecipeExtractPdfImages`, CoreGraphics PDF structure: JPEG as is, JPEG 2000 and decoded 8-bit RGB/grey re-encoded as JPEG, with size, page and mask flag). A picture guessed from a rendered page is never chosen on its own (`noAuto`). A page-sized embedded picture is a scan: the dish photo is looked for on it.
- **Photographed or scanned pages:** the page is straightened first, then searched for the printed dish photo (previously the original picture was searched, where the table around the page could be taken for a picture). The photo is offered as the cover with a "check it" notice until adjusted, replaced or approved. The original is never changed.
- **Frame:** the whole picture is stored; the recipe stores only `photoFrame` = `{ zoom, cx, cy }` (zoom 1-4, centre point as fractions), so the same framing fits any shape (cover, thumbnail, planner tile). Editor: a fixed 16:10 frame, the whole picture behind it dimmed outside the frame, drag to move, pinch / wheel / slider / + - to zoom, no empty edges, Reset, Replace, Cancel, Done. The framing is saved with the recipe (and its draft), reopens exactly as left, and is applied on the recipe page, the collection and the planner. Tests: `tests/photoframe.test.mjs`, `tools/e2e-photo.cjs`.
- **Import review / Rescue / recipe page:** one photo flow (`screens/photo.js`): choose (candidates, library, camera, cut out of the original), then frame. The import review's photo tap, Recipe Rescue and the recipe page all use it.
- **Detection reliability (causes found in the old code):** (1) the filename filter rejected any picture whose name merely CONTAINED a junk word (`Honey-Mustard-Chicken.jpg` contains "star"; `Sharing-Pie`, `blueprint`, `Starbucks`, ...): now whole words only; (2) only the first three web pictures were tried and one failed download meant "no photo": now up to six are looked at, failures and non-pictures are skipped; (3) `<picture><source srcset>` was ignored: now read; (4) a PDF page picture was searched for a "photo" and an advert was taken: see above.

## Limits (said plainly)
- Native PDF extraction handles JPEG, JPEG 2000 and 8-bit RGB / grey pictures; CMYK, indexed or 16-bit pictures are skipped (not offered). It is compiled and probed on a macOS runner only; not seen on a device.
- "Finished dish" cannot be recognised by looking at a picture without a trained model; the rules use position, size, colour and where the picture came from. An advert without a mask and with an ordinary shape could be chosen: it is always shown for a check and listed beside the others.
- Candidate pictures live in the app session; a draft resumed after the app was closed offers only the dish photo kept with its original, the library and the camera.
- The Android app is not changed (iOS JavaScript only).
