# Shared Component Registry — Required Discovery

Authoritative catalog: https://github.com/mrmundysr-sudo/Touch-Modules/tree/main/registry
Index: https://github.com/mrmundysr-sudo/Touch-Modules/blob/main/registry/components.json

Before substantial UI, networking, media, persistence or build work:
1. Read the registry and relevant documentation.
2. Inspect source at the recorded repository/path/revision, license, dependencies, compatibility, limitations and tests.
3. Prefer compatible reusable components; document why a candidate is unsuitable.
4. Do not assume a component works outside its source app without integration validation.
5. Propose reusable additions with evidence after validation.
6. Do not modify other repositories or extract cross-repo modules without explicit approval.

Also inspect appropriate Android libraries for recipe structured-data parsing, HTML/document handling, OCR and accessibility, with license and terms review. Registry entries are a catalog, not proof of packaged code.
